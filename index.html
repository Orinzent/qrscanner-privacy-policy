Privacy Policy

Application: QR Scanner | Package: com.mecorven.qrscanner
Key Privacy Summary: QR Scanner is designed with a privacy-first approach. All scanning, decoding, and code generation processes occur locally on your device. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, camera feeds, or scan history to remote servers.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how the QR Scanner mobile application ("the App", "we", "our") handles your information. The application is built to serve as a fast, reliable, and secure barcode and QR code scanner and generator. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through transparent data handling practices.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect or harvest any personally identifiable information (PII).

The App does not require account creation, registration, email sign-ups, or personal identity verification. You can use all functionalities of the application anonymously.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

To provide standard scanning and generation features, the App requests specific device permissions. These permissions are strictly used for their stated on-device functionality:

Permission Purpose & Scope
Camera
(android.permission.CAMERA)		 Used exclusively in real-time to detect and decode QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are processed strictly in memory and are discarded immediately. No photos, videos, or visual feeds are saved, recorded, or transmitted to any server.
Storage / Photos
(READ_MEDIA_IMAGES)		 Used only when you explicitly choose to scan a barcode or QR code from an existing image in your device gallery or save a newly generated code to your device storage.
Flashlight
(android.permission.FLASHLIGHT)		 Enables toggling the device torch to illuminate barcodes and QR codes in low-light scanning environments.
Vibration / Haptics
(android.permission.VIBRATE)		 Provides non-intrusive tactile vibration feedback when a code is successfully scanned.

4. Local Storage and Data Retention

Any data generated while using the App—including your scan history, favorite items, custom created codes, and settings preferences—is saved locally on your physical device using private app storage.

  • You retain full ownership and control over your history data.
  • You can clear individual history items or the entire scan history at any time directly through the in-app settings.
  • Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all locally stored data created by the App.

5. External Links and Third-Party Interaction

When scanning QR codes containing external URLs or web links, the App will display the link for your verification. If you choose to open a link, it will be handled by your device's default web browser or appropriate application. We do not track, analyze, or log the external websites you visit or the actions you perform on third-party sites.

6. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The App does not integrate third-party advertising tracking networks or user tracking SDKs that profile your behavior across applications.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is safe for general audiences and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in applicable jurisdictions), in full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy frameworks.

8. Security

Because all processing is performed locally on your device without transmitting your scan contents or images over the Internet, your data remains within the boundaries of your device's operating system security.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
